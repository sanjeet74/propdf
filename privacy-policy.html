Privacy Policy

Propdf (Android app) · ShwetaTech · Effective date: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Propdf app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is developed by ShwetaTech. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Your PDFs, scans and images are processed on your device. We do not upload them to any server, and we cannot see them.
  • We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.

2. Files you open, edit and create

When you open a PDF, choose images, or scan a document, the App only gets access to the specific files you pick through the Android file picker or photo picker. The App does not scan your storage and does not access any file you did not select.

Edits (text, images, highlights, merging, splitting, image-to-PDF conversion) are performed locally. Temporary working copies are kept in the App's private cache on your device and are deleted automatically as you work, or when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Files are saved only to the location you choose.

3. Document scanner

The scan feature uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, provided by Google Play services. The camera is opened and controlled by Google Play services, not by the App, and the scanned pages are processed on your device and returned to the App as a PDF. Google Play services may download the scanner module and may collect limited diagnostic and usage information in line with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads (app open ads and optional rewarded video ads) served by Google AdMob. To deliver, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, clicks, whether a rewarded video was completed) and diagnostic data such as crash logs

This data is collected by Google, not stored by us. Learn more at How Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob and privacy.

5. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) where you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the App's menu under Privacy settings.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your device).
  • Rewarded videos are always optional: the App asks before playing one, and you can cancel.

6. Data we share

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform and Google Play services), for the purposes described above.

7. Data security and retention

Because your documents never leave your device, their security depends on your device. Data transmitted to Google for ads is encrypted in transit. We do not keep any server-side copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes all of its local data.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to load ads and the consent form.
  • Opening files and photos uses Android's system file and photo pickers, which give the App access only to the items you choose.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
